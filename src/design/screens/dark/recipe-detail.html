Pasta Carbonara

+ Pancetta 150g Buy
+ Pecorino Romano 50g Buy
✓ Garlic 2 cloves Have

Instructions

1 Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
2 While pasta cooks, cut pancetta into small strips. Cook in a cold skillet over medium heat until crispy and fat renders, about 6-8 minutes.
3 In a bowl, whisk eggs with finely grated Pecorino Romano and Parmesan until smooth. Season generously with black pepper.
4 Remove skillet from heat. Add drained pasta to the pancetta and toss. Let cool for 1 minute, then pour egg mixture over pasta while tossing vigorously.
5 Add reserved pasta water a little at a time until sauce is creamy and coats each strand. Serve immediately with extra Pecorino and pepper.
